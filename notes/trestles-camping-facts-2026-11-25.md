## A. Reaching Trestles (San Onofre SB)
Source: https://www.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=647 ("San Onofre State Beach") and infosheet PDF https://www.parks.ca.gov/pages/647/files/SanOnofreSB_Infosheet_8.5x14_060926_FINAL.pdf ("San Onofre State Beach Map and Guide")
- CONFIRMED: Trestles cannot be reached by car. Only on foot via a nature trail from the north or south end, passing under the Trestles Bridge.
- CONFIRMED: A 1.5-mile Nature Trail connects San Mateo Campground to Trestles. Infosheet suggests walking from the campground to Trestles as a full-day plan.
- CONFIRMED: San Mateo Campground address 830 Cristianitos Road, San Clemente 92672; about 1 mile inland from I-5 at the Cristianitos exit. Campground hours 6 am-10 pm.
- CONFIRMED: Infosheet calls Trestles a professional-level break with consistent waves, a favorite of experienced surfers. Surf Beach is the gentler, cobblestone-reef break (200-400 yds offshore), suitable for all levels; Bluffs ("Trails") is the southern, less crowded break.
- CONFIRMED: Day-use parking $15 regular, $20 holiday/summer weekends per vehicle. Vehicles over 25 ft not allowed at Surf Beach. Passes accepted: Disabled Discount, Distinguished Veteran, Explorer, Golden Bear, Senior Golden Bear, State Library.
- CONFIRMED: Surf Beach hours 6 am-10 pm (Apr 15 onward in summer season) and 6 am-8 pm Oct 1-Apr 14 (page has typo "9/31"; say "through the summer season"). Bluffs/trails 6 am-sunset.
- CONFIRMED rules: alcohol prohibited unless special event permit; bonfires only in State Parks fire rings, first come first served; drones/remote aerial devices prohibited (infosheet); dogs not on sand, in water or most trails, only Bluffs Trail 1 and Trail 6 on max 6 ft leash, service animals allowed everywhere; stay 10 ft from bluffs, visit at low tide for more sand.
- CONFIRMED: Infosheet says Bluffs Beach Trails 2, 3, 5 closed (infosheet June 2026 version; not re-verified as current).
- CONFIRMED: Surf Beach facilities: chemical toilets, cold showers, limited fire pits (main page).
- CONFIRMED accessibility https://www.parks.ca.gov/AccessibleFeatures/Details/647 : San Mateo has 3 accessible tent sites + 2 RV; restrooms/showers generally accessible; campfire center accessible. Free beach wheelchair at Surf Beach: peak season Jun 1-Sep 15 call lifeguard tower (949) 366-8592 daily; off season call on weekends. Page has nothing on the Trestles trail itself (do not claim it is accessible).
- Park phone (949) 492-4872.

## B. San Clemente State Beach
Source: https://www.parks.ca.gov/sanClemente ("San Clemente State Beach")
- CONFIRMED: Park hours 6 am-10 pm; visitor center 10 am-4 pm Sun-Fri (closed most Saturdays Mar-Nov for events).
- CONFIRMED day use per vehicle: $15 regular; $30 oversize (25 ft+); Memorial Day-Labor Day weekends/holidays $20 regular, $40 oversize; senior 62+ $14 (regular); fees can change.
- CONFIRMED camping per night: RV with hookups $70; tent $45-$50; group $250-$300; extra vehicle $15; dump station $10; reservation fee $8.25; seniors 62+ $2 off online; max trailer/motorhome 30 ft. (Re-verified: the earlier $70 and $45-50 figures hold.)
- CONFIRMED: one-mile beach below sandstone cliffs; exit Avenida Calafia off I-5; steep trails to beach; wheelchair help call (949) 204-5803.
- CONFIRMED amenities: tent/RV/group sites, hookups, dump station, parking, restrooms, showers, drinking water, picnic areas, visitor center, fishing, surfing, swimming, snorkel/scuba, EV chargers.
- CONFIRMED rules: bonfires only in designated rings; alcohol prohibited without special event permit; drones prohibited without film permit; tents must be open on at least one side; dogs not on sand or in water (service animals excepted), leashed dogs allowed on multi-use trail and parking lots (page also has a contradictory summary line, so tell readers to confirm with the park); surf fishing needs license 16+.
- CONFIRMED accessibility https://www.parks.ca.gov/AccessibleFeatures/Details/646: 9 accessible tent sites (+4 designated), 3 accessible RV hookup sites (#20, #54, #56); beach wheelchairs at entrance kiosk; north beach access trail is the less steep route; staff can transport; phone (949) 492-3156.
- CONFIRMED beach trail link: 2.3-mile multi-use trail North Beach to Calafia Beach via the Pier and T-Street (state page and city page).
- CONFIRMED (generic State Parks policy) https://www.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=26835 ("Campground Information & Regulations"): check-in 2:00 pm, no exceptions; early arrivals may use day-use parking for a fee; reservations from same day to 6 months ahead; gates lock nightly, no vehicles in or out; RVs back in.
- CONFIRMED https://www.parks.ca.gov/31966 ("Booking and Fees"): rolling 6-month window, inventory released daily 8 am PST; most parks take reservations until 2 days before arrival; $8.25 reservation fee, non-refundable; $8.25 cancellation fee; some high-demand parks use drawings up to 8 months ahead (not said to include San Clemente).

## C. San Onofre camping
- CONFIRMED (main page): San Mateo Campground camping year-round, RV hookups (electric, water), campfire program; fire pit at every site. Bluffs campground: fire pit + picnic table each site, no RV hookups, max length 36 ft, cold outdoor showers, chemical toilets, dump station; Bluffs entrance locked 10 pm-6 am. Surf Beach is day use only. Main page says camping at Bluffs "when available" and lists a Bluffs restrictions advisory, so tell readers to check availability.

## D. City of San Clemente Beach Trail
Sources: https://www.sanclemente.gov/Facilities/Facility/Details/Beach-Trail-75 ("Facilities - Beach Trail"); https://www.sanclemente.gov/194/Beaches ("Beaches", read via search summary only)
- CONFIRMED: 2.3 miles, North Beach to Calafia Beach via Pier and T-Street. Hours 4 am-midnight; South T-Street restrooms to Calafia 6 am-10 pm. Dogs leashed 6 ft max, not on the beach. Bikes yield to pedestrians, under 10 mph; e-bikes of any class and motorized vehicles banned. No smoking. Use designated rail crossings; stay out of OCTA rail corridor.
- CONFIRMED (search summary of Beaches page): ADA access at North Beach, Pier, Linda Lane; stairs at Dije Court, El Portal, Mariposa, Lasuen, Calafia.

## E. Olympics
Source: https://www.parks.ca.gov/newsrelease/1372 ("Surf's Up! Surfing Competition at LA28 Olympics Coming to San Onofre State Beach", Apr 15 2025)
- CONFIRMED: LA28 announced Trestles Beach will host Olympic surfing at the 2028 Summer Games; release says to mark July 2028. Trestles about 2.5 million visitors a year. State Parks has operated the park since 1971 under agreement with the Navy; renewed for 25 years Aug 2024. Site sacred to the Acjachemen. State Parks says it will work with LA28 and others to prepare Trestles Beach. Release does not describe access changes: do not speculate.
